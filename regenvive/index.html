What Is the RegenVive Supplement?

RegenVive is a dietary supplement in capsule form that's marketed to support healthy blood sugar levels, insulin sensitivity and steady energy.* Each bottle holds 60 capsules.

Instead of a single active ingredient, the formula combines six botanicals that have been used in traditional herbal practice for generations, several of which have been studied for their effect on glucose metabolism.

It's aimed at adults who want extra support for balanced blood sugar alongside a healthy diet — for example people who notice energy crashes after meals or frequent sugar cravings. It is not a medicine and isn't meant to treat diabetes.

How RegenVive Works for Blood Sugar

The formula's ingredients target three things that affect how your body handles sugar every day.*

1

Supports Insulin Sensitivity

Insulin moves sugar from your blood into your cells. Banaba and bitter melon contain compounds studied for helping cells take up glucose.*

2

Slows Sugar Absorption

White mulberry and gymnema may slow how quickly carbohydrates are absorbed after a meal, supporting fewer spikes and crashes.*

3

Antioxidant Support

Juniper berry and guggul provide antioxidants that help protect cells from everyday oxidative stress linked to metabolic health.*

RegenVive Ingredients

Inside each capsule you'll find these six plant ingredients.

Banaba Leaf

Contains corosolic acid, which has been studied for its role in helping cells use glucose more efficiently.*

Guggul

A tree resin used in Ayurveda, traditionally taken to support healthy cholesterol and metabolic balance.*

Bitter Melon

A tropical gourd with plant compounds that may act similarly to insulin and support glucose uptake.*

Gymnema Sylvestre

Known in Hindi as “gurmar” (sugar destroyer); it may reduce the taste of sweetness and help curb sugar cravings.*

Juniper Berry

A rich source of antioxidants that has been studied for supporting pancreatic and overall cell health.*

White Mulberry

Leaf extract that may slow carbohydrate breakdown in the gut, supporting steadier blood sugar after meals.*

RegenVive Side Effects

The ingredients are plant extracts that most healthy adults tolerate well. Still, there are a few things to know before you start:

  • Digestive upset. Mild bloating or an upset stomach is possible, especially in the first days. Taking the capsules with food usually helps.
  • Low blood sugar risk. Bitter melon, gymnema and banaba can lower blood sugar. If you take diabetes medication or insulin, talk to your doctor first so your levels don't drop too low.
  • Medication interactions. Guggul may interact with thyroid medication and blood thinners. Check with your pharmacist if you take any prescription drugs.
  • Who should avoid it. Don't use RegenVive if you're pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or scheduled for surgery within two weeks.

If you notice dizziness, shakiness or any unusual reaction, stop taking it and contact your doctor.
