About MediPicker

Last updated: October 23, 2026

MediPicker is an independent website that reviews health supplements sold online — the kind usually promoted through long video sales letters and "official website" pages. We are not a manufacturer, seller or distributor of any product we write about.

Why we started

Supplement ads often make big promises, hide the ingredient amounts and send shoppers to look-alike websites. We wanted one place where people can quickly see what a product actually contains, what it costs, what the refund policy really says and whether the claims match the evidence — before they spend money.

What we cover

  • Product directory and short reviews for more than 100 supplements, grouped by health topic — weight loss, blood sugar, hearing, dental, vision, joints, men's and women's health and more.
  • In-depth reviews on product sub-sites (for example nervealive.medipicker.com) that go through the full label, the research on each ingredient, side effects, prices and the money-back guarantee.

How we write our reviews

Every in-depth review starts from the seller's own order page and the product's supplement facts label, then compares the ingredients and doses with published research. We point out when a label doesn't match the sales page, when doses aren't disclosed, when ads make claims the evidence doesn't support, and when an ingredient carries a safety warning. You can read the full process on our How We Review page.

Who we are

MediPicker is run by a small independent research and editorial team. We are not doctors, and our reviews are not medical advice. We summarise publicly available information — product labels, sellers' pages and published studies — in plain language, and we always recommend talking to your doctor or pharmacist before starting a supplement, especially if you take medication.

Our independence

MediPicker is free to read, and no manufacturer or seller decides what we write. We publish ingredient problems, missing labels, interaction warnings and weak evidence for every product we cover, including the ones we link to, and we never accept payment for positive reviews or better verdicts.

What we will never do

  • Pretend to be the official website or the manufacturer.
  • Publish fake customer reviews, ratings or "doctor endorsements".
  • Claim a supplement is FDA-approved or can cure a disease.

Contact and corrections

Found an error, an outdated price or a changed label? Email us at info@medipicker.com. We review every correction request and update the page — and its "Updated" date — when the information has changed.
