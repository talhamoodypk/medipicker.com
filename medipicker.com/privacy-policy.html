Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MediPicker ("we", "us") handles information when you visit medipicker.com and its review pages.

Information we collect

We don't ask you to create an account or submit personal information. Our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, which is used only to keep the site secure and working.

Cookies

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies.

Google Ads measurement

Some review pages that we promote with Google Ads load the Google Ads tag (Google's gtag.js). It sets cookies so Google can tell us whether a visitor who arrived from one of our ads then clicked through to a seller's website. We use this only to measure which ads are useful; we don't use it to build audiences or show you ads based on your health. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn off personalised ads in your Google ad settings or block cookies in your browser.

Links to other websites

Buy Now buttons take you to the official websites of the products listed. Those websites and their payment processors have their own privacy policies, which apply to any information you give them — including payment details. We never see or store your payment information.

Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
