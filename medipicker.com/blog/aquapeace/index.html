What Is AquaPeace?

AquaPeace is a once-a-day 'deep sea hearing formula' made from marine nutrients: algae, seaweed and astaxanthin. It's sold to support ear health and, according to testimonials, better sleep.

AquaPeace Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“This Deep Sea Hearing Formula Supports The Health Of Your Ears”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • The hearing references are animal studies (for example a seaweed compound protecting hearing in mice); there are no human trials.
  • Astaxanthin and algae have small human studies for other uses (eye strain, cholesterol), not hearing.
  • Seaweed adds iodine, which matters for people with thyroid disease.
  • 'Supply chains are affected' is used to push larger orders.

AquaPeace Ingredients

The official website names 4 key ingredients in AquaPeace. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Chlorella

Chlorella is a green freshwater alga sold as a nutrient supplement. Small studies suggest modest effects on cholesterol and blood pressure; there's no evidence for vision.

Spirulina

Spirulina is a blue-green alga sold as a protein- and nutrient-rich 'superfood'. Small studies suggest modest effects on cholesterol; evidence for immunity is limited.

Nori Yaki

Nori is the red seaweed used to wrap sushi. It's a food source of iodine and protein; there's no evidence for prostate health.

Astaxanthin

Astaxanthin is a red carotenoid from algae that gives salmon its color. Small trials suggest 6–12 mg a day may reduce eye strain and improve focusing in people who use screens a lot.

The page also mentions omega oils and seaweed compounds such as fucoxanthin, fucoidan and phlorotannins in its references. Amounts aren't disclosed.

Is AquaPeace Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • See a doctor or audiologist about sudden hearing loss, ear pain or new ringing — these need medical attention.
  • Check with your doctor if you take blood thinners, as some plant extracts can affect clotting.
  • Supplements are not a replacement for hearing aids or medical treatment.
  • Spirulina from untested sources can be contaminated with toxins; it isn't suitable for people with autoimmune disease or PKU.
