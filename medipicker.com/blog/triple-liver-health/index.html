Triple Liver Health Ingredients

The official website names 9 key ingredients in Triple Liver Health. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Milk Thistle (Silymarin)

Milk thistle seed extract contains silymarin, the most widely used liver-support herb. Trials in fatty liver disease and hepatitis show small or inconsistent improvements in liver enzymes; it's generally well tolerated.

Choline

Choline is a nutrient needed to transport fat out of the liver. Deficiency causes fatty liver, but there's no clear evidence that extra choline helps people who already get enough from eggs, meat and fish.

Artichoke Leaf

Artichoke leaf extract is used for indigestion and cholesterol. Small trials show modest relief of indigestion and slight cholesterol reductions.

Beetroot

Beetroot is rich in nitrates, which the body turns into nitric oxide. Beetroot juice can modestly lower blood pressure and help exercise performance; there's no evidence for skin.

Chanca Piedra

Chanca piedra (Phyllanthus niruri), 'stone breaker', is a South American herb traditionally used for kidney stones. A few small studies suggest it may help pass small kidney stones; there is no evidence for skin.

Dandelion Root

Dandelion root and leaf are traditional digestive and diuretic herbs. Human evidence is limited to a few small studies; dandelion leaf has a mild diuretic effect.

Chicory Root

Chicory root is a source of inulin fiber. As a prebiotic fiber it feeds gut bacteria; evidence for liver health is limited.

Yarrow

Yarrow is a traditional herb used for digestion and wounds. There is no meaningful clinical evidence for liver health.

Jujube Seed

Jujube seed is used in traditional Chinese medicine for sleep and calm. Evidence is mostly from animal studies.

The website names these ingredients 'and a lot of more natural' ones, without amounts.
