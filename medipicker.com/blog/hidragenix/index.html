Hidragenix Ingredients

The official website names 5 key ingredients in Hidragenix. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Capsicum Annuum (Chili Pepper)

Capsicum is chili pepper extract, a source of capsaicin. Capsaicin has small effects on metabolism and appetite; there's no evidence it helps hearing.

Forskolin (Coleus forskohlii)

Forskolin is a compound from the coleus plant that raises cAMP in cells. Two small trials showed mixed results on body fat and no clear weight loss.

Grape Seed Extract

Grape seed extract is rich in antioxidant proanthocyanidins. It may modestly lower blood pressure; protection against hearing loss has only been shown in animal studies.

Gymnema Sylvestre

Gymnema is an Ayurvedic herb known as 'sugar destroyer' because it blocks the taste of sweetness. Small trials suggest modest reductions in blood sugar in people with diabetes; quality is low.

Green Tea Extract

Green tea extract supplies catechins, mainly EGCG, and usually some caffeine. Studies show small effects on energy expenditure and fat oxidation, especially combined with caffeine; weight loss in trials is modest (around 1 kg).

The letter names five plant extracts; amounts aren't disclosed.

Is Hidragenix Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Some weight management formulas contain caffeine or green tea extract — avoid taking them late in the day if you're sensitive.
  • Talk to your doctor first if you take medication for diabetes, blood pressure or your heart.
  • Don't use weight-loss supplements while pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Gymnema can lower blood sugar and may add to the effect of diabetes medicine.
  • Concentrated green tea extract has been linked to rare cases of liver injury, especially when taken on an empty stomach; it also contains caffeine.
