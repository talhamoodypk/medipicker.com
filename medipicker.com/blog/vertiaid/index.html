What Is VertiAid?

VertiAid is a chewable capsule sold for balance and dizziness, based on the idea that supporting the 'membrane' of the inner ear improves balance. Its formula is built on collagen.

VertiAid Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“A Breakthrough Solution That Supports Balance By Maintaining The Health Of Your Inner Ear's Membrane”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • The inner ear's balance organs are filled with fluid and lined with membranes, but there's no evidence that eating collagen reaches or repairs them.
  • The most common cause of vertigo (BPPV) is loose crystals in the inner ear, which is fixed in minutes with a repositioning maneuver.
  • Eggshell membrane has small studies for knee joint pain, not balance.
  • Bonus claims about chocolate and wine for hearing aren't evidence-based.

VertiAid Ingredients

The official website names 6 key ingredients in VertiAid. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Eggshell Membrane (Eggshell Collagen)

Eggshell membrane is the thin layer inside eggshells, containing collagen, elastin and hyaluronic acid. Small trials suggest it may modestly reduce knee joint pain and stiffness; there's no evidence for the inner ear or balance.

Collagen

Collagen supplements provide protein fragments (peptides) from animal skin, bones or fish. Trials suggest collagen peptides may modestly improve skin elasticity and joint pain; the body breaks them down, so they don't travel to a specific organ like the inner ear.

Zinc-L-Carnosine

Zinc-L-carnosine (polaprezinc) is a compound used in Japan for stomach ulcers. It has evidence for protecting the stomach lining; there's no evidence for balance or the inner ear.

Pineapple Powder (Bromelain)

Pineapple contains bromelain, a protein-digesting enzyme. Bromelain has small studies for reducing swelling after surgery or injury, usually at concentrated doses.

Ginseng

Ginseng (Panax) root is a traditional energy and vitality herb. Some trials show small improvements in fatigue and erectile-function scores in people; evidence quality is mixed.

Papaya Powder

Papaya contains papain, a protein-digesting enzyme. It's used as a digestive aid; there's no evidence for balance.

The blend lists pure eggshell collagen (eggshell membrane) and marine collagen in a proprietary blend; amounts aren't disclosed.

Is VertiAid Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • See a doctor about dizziness or vertigo — it can have causes that need medical care.
  • Check with your doctor if you take medication.
  • Take extra care with falls risk while trying any new product.
  • Eggshell membrane isn't suitable for people with egg allergies.
  • Bromelain may increase bleeding risk with blood thinners.
  • Ginseng can affect blood sugar and blood pressure and interacts with warfarin.

Our Verdict on VertiAid

VertiAid's theory doesn't match how vertigo works. Many causes of dizziness are quickly treatable: BPPV with the Epley maneuver, vestibular neuritis with rehabilitation, and Ménière's disease with specialist care. See a doctor, and get urgent help if dizziness comes with weakness, slurred speech or a severe headache.
