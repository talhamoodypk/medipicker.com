What Is Cleanest Body?

Cleanest Body is a liquid 'Mayan cleanse' added daily to juice, tea or water, sold for weight loss and gut health.

Its ingredient list is identical to GlucoFlush, which the same style of page sells for blood sugar: a herbal parasite-cleanse blend of fennel, marshmallow root, pumpkin seed, slippery elm, clove, garlic, oregano oil, peppermint oil and papaya seed.

Cleanest Body Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“This Mayan Cleanse Supports A Healthy Weight And Gut”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • None of the ingredients has evidence for weight loss.
  • Parasites aren't a common cause of weight gain in developed countries.
  • The same formula is sold for blood sugar under another name.
  • Separating it from medicines by two hours is sensible, because mucilage herbs can slow drug absorption.

Cleanest Body Ingredients

The official website names 9 key ingredients in Cleanest Body. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Fennel Seed

Fennel seed is a traditional remedy for gas and bloating. There is limited evidence for infant colic and digestion, and none for nail health.

Marshmallow Root

Marshmallow root contains mucilage, a gel-forming fiber that coats the throat and gut. It's used for sore throats and coughs; there's no evidence for blood sugar.

Pumpkin Seed Extract

Pumpkin seed provides zinc, plant sterols and healthy fats. Some trials suggest pumpkin seed extract helps urinary symptoms from an enlarged prostate; there's no evidence for nails or feet.

Slippery Elm Bark

Slippery elm is a mucilage-rich bark used to soothe the digestive tract. Evidence is limited to traditional use and very small studies.

Clove Bud Oil

Clove oil is rich in eugenol, a numbing and antiseptic compound. It shows antifungal activity in lab tests; clinical evidence on nails is lacking.

Garlic

Garlic contains allicin and other sulfur compounds. Garlic supplements modestly lower blood pressure and cholesterol in trials; effects on blood sugar are small.

Oregano Essential Oil

Oregano oil contains carvacrol and thymol, which kill fungi in lab studies. There are no good clinical trials on nail fungus.

Peppermint Oil

Peppermint oil relaxes the smooth muscle of the gut. Enteric-coated peppermint oil capsules have good evidence for irritable bowel syndrome symptoms; there's no evidence for blood sugar.

Papaya Seed Extract

Papaya seeds are a traditional remedy for intestinal worms. One small Nigerian study suggested an effect on intestinal parasites; there's no evidence for blood sugar.

Amounts aren't disclosed.

Is Cleanest Body Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Fiber and probiotic products can cause gas or bloating at first.
  • See a doctor about ongoing stomach pain, blood in the stool or unexplained weight loss.
  • Check with your doctor if you have a digestive condition or take medication.
  • Mucilage herbs like slippery elm and marshmallow can slow the absorption of medicines; take them apart.
  • Garlic supplements can increase bleeding risk with blood thinners.
  • Peppermint oil can worsen heartburn and reflux.
