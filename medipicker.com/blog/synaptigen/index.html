What Is Synaptigen?

Synaptigen is a memory supplement built on probiotics: a melt-in-the-mouth tablet with three probiotic strains, prebiotic fiber and peppermint. It's based on the gut-brain axis idea, that gut bacteria influence brain health.

Synaptigen Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“A Breakthrough Natural Solution That Supports A Healthy Memory Well Into Old Age”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • The gut-brain axis is a real research area; a few small trials of specific probiotic strains showed modest improvements in memory test scores.
  • These results depend on the exact strain and dose; Synaptigen's L. paracasei and L. reuteri strains aren't specified.
  • No probiotic has been shown to prevent dementia.
  • Melt-in-the-mouth tablets may be a poor way to deliver probiotics, which need to survive to the gut.

Synaptigen Ingredients

The official website names 5 key ingredients in Synaptigen. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Lactobacillus paracasei

L. paracasei is a probiotic bacterium found in fermented foods. Some strains have small studies for stress and digestion; effects are strain-specific.

Lactobacillus reuteri

L. reuteri is a probiotic bacterium found in the human gut and breast milk. Specific strains (such as DSM 17938) have good evidence for infant colic; memory evidence is early.

Bifidobacterium lactis BL-04

B. lactis BL-04 is a specific, well-characterized probiotic strain. It has been studied for digestive and immune health, including respiratory infections in athletes; evidence for memory is lacking.

Inulin

Inulin is a prebiotic fiber, usually from chicory root, that feeds gut bacteria. It can increase bifidobacteria and soften stools at several grams a day; there's no evidence it improves gum health.

Peppermint Oil

Peppermint oil relaxes the smooth muscle of the gut. Enteric-coated peppermint oil capsules have good evidence for irritable bowel syndrome symptoms; there's no evidence for blood sugar.

One strain is named precisely (B. lactis BL-04), which is better than most products, but CFU counts are not disclosed.

Is Synaptigen Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • See a doctor or audiologist about sudden hearing loss, ear pain or new ringing — these need medical attention.
  • Check with your doctor if you take blood thinners, as some plant extracts can affect clotting.
  • Supplements are not a replacement for hearing aids or medical treatment.
  • Inulin and other prebiotic fibers commonly cause gas and bloating, especially at first.
  • Peppermint oil can worsen heartburn and reflux.
