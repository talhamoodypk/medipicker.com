What Is ReFirmance?

ReFirmance is a facial serum applied as drops to the face and neck, sold to support collagen and firmer, less saggy skin. The sales page says it has a proprietary formula of 17 skin-supporting ingredients.

The sales story says collagen fragments 'jam' an enzyme called HAS2, so hyaluronic acid pools in the skin and causes sagging. HAS2 (hyaluronan synthase 2) is a real enzyme, but this sagging explanation is a simplification that isn't established in dermatology research.

ReFirmance Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“The Natural "Lift Serum"”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • Palmitoyl peptides (as in Matrixyl) have small studies showing modest improvements in fine lines; effects are far smaller than retinoids or professional treatments.
  • Chrysin is sometimes included for under-eye puffiness, with very limited evidence.
  • No serum can 'lift' sagging skin the way the name suggests; sagging is caused by loss of collagen, fat and bone structure over time.
  • The claim that all ingredients 'have been proven to be safe in clinical trials' isn't backed by any cited trial of the product.

ReFirmance Ingredients

The official website names 6 key ingredients in ReFirmance. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Palmitoyl Oligopeptide

Palmitoyl oligopeptide is a signal peptide used in anti-aging creams (part of the Matrixyl 3000 complex). Small, often manufacturer-funded studies show modest reductions in fine lines; the effect is smaller than with retinoids.

Chrysin

Chrysin is a flavonoid from passionflower and honey, used in some eye creams. It's included to reduce under-eye puffiness and dark circles, but the evidence is very limited.

Micrococcus Lysate

Micrococcus lysate is a bacterial extract marketed as a DNA-repair enzyme for skin. Evidence comes mostly from manufacturer and lab studies; it is not a substitute for sunscreen.

Aloe Vera

Aloe vera gel and latex come from the leaves of the aloe plant. Aloe latex is a strong stimulant laxative; the inner gel has limited, mixed evidence for irritable bowel symptoms.

Cucumber Extract

Cucumber extract is a soothing, hydrating cosmetic ingredient. It can feel cooling and moisturizing; it doesn't firm or lift skin.

Glycerin

Glycerin is a humectant that draws water into the outer layer of the skin. It's one of the most effective, well-proven moisturizing ingredients, found in most skincare products.

The ingredient list also shows deionized water, carbomer (a thickener) and phenoxyethanol (a preservative), which is a standard cosmetic serum base. Concentrations are not disclosed.

  • Do a patch test before using any new oil or cream on your skin.
  • See a doctor about persistent fungal infections, rashes or sudden hair loss.
  • Stop use if you notice irritation.
  • Aloe latex is a stimulant laxative and isn't suitable for long-term daily use or during pregnancy.
