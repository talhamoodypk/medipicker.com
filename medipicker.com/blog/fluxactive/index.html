Fluxactive Ingredients

The official website names 11 key ingredients in Fluxactive. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Saw Palmetto

Saw palmetto berry extract is the most popular herbal remedy for an enlarged prostate (BPH). Early small trials looked promising, but the large, well-run STEP and CAMUS trials and a Cochrane review found it worked no better than placebo for urinary symptoms, even at triple doses.

Ginseng

Ginseng (Panax) root is a traditional energy and vitality herb. Some trials show small improvements in fatigue and erectile-function scores in people; evidence quality is mixed.

Damiana

Damiana is a Mexican shrub traditionally used as an aphrodisiac. Human evidence is very limited; there are no prostate trials.

Horny Goat Weed (Epimedium)

Horny goat weed contains icariin, a compound that weakly blocks the PDE5 enzyme in lab studies. There are almost no good human trials on erections; lab potency is far weaker than prescription drugs.

Ginkgo Biloba

Ginkgo leaf extract, usually standardized to 24% flavone glycosides, is one of the most-studied herbal memory supplements. Results are mixed: some trials show small benefits in people with dementia, but the large GEM trial of over 3,000 older adults found it did not prevent dementia or cognitive decline.

Oat Straw

Oat straw is the green stem of the oat plant. One small study suggested it may help attention in older adults; there's no evidence for prostate health.

Muira Puama

Muira puama is a Brazilian bark traditionally used as an aphrodisiac. Human evidence is limited to a few old, low-quality studies.

Inosine

Inosine is a building block of RNA, sold to athletes for energy. Trials found no performance benefit; there's no evidence for prostate health.

Cayenne Pepper

Cayenne contains capsaicin, the compound that makes chili peppers hot. Capsaicin slightly raises energy use and may reduce appetite in studies, but the effects are small; it can also irritate a sensitive stomach.

Vitamin E

Vitamin E is a fat-soluble antioxidant vitamin. The large SELECT trial found vitamin E supplements slightly increased prostate cancer risk in men, and it has shown no clear benefit for memory in healthy adults.

Niacin (Vitamin B3)

Niacin is a B vitamin used at high doses to lower cholesterol. Nicotinamide (a form of B3) is being studied for glaucoma; regular niacin has no proven vision benefit.

The page names these ingredients among the 14 in the blend; amounts are not given.

Is Fluxactive Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Talk to your doctor before use if you take blood pressure, heart or prostate medication.
  • See a doctor about any new urinary symptoms — they can have causes that need medical care.
  • Don't exceed the recommended dose on the label.
  • Ginseng can affect blood sugar and blood pressure and interacts with warfarin.
  • Ginkgo may increase bleeding risk; avoid combining it with blood thinners or aspirin and stop it before surgery.
  • Cayenne/capsaicin can cause heartburn or stomach irritation in sensitive people.
  • High-dose niacin causes flushing and, rarely, liver problems and swelling of the retina.
