What Is EndoPeak?

EndoPeak is a male-enhancement capsule sold for stronger, faster erections. It combines traditional 'libido' herbs (horny goat weed, tribulus and tongkat ali) with hawthorn, saw palmetto and magnesium.

EndoPeak Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“Erection Muscle Trick Gets You Hard in 9 Seconds”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • No supplement produces an erection 'in 9 seconds'; even prescription PDE5 drugs take 30–60 minutes.
  • Tongkat ali raised testosterone and improved sexual-function scores in some small trials; evidence is limited but positive.
  • Tribulus has repeatedly failed to raise testosterone in men, and its effect on erections is unclear.
  • Horny goat weed's icariin works like a very weak PDE5 inhibitor in the lab; human evidence is minimal.

EndoPeak Ingredients

The official website names 6 key ingredients in EndoPeak. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Horny Goat Weed (Epimedium)

Horny goat weed contains icariin, a compound that weakly blocks the PDE5 enzyme in lab studies. There are almost no good human trials on erections; lab potency is far weaker than prescription drugs.

Tribulus Terrestris

Tribulus is a plant widely sold for testosterone and libido. Multiple trials found it does not raise testosterone in men; a few small studies suggest slight improvements in sexual-function scores.

Tongkat Ali (Eurycoma longifolia)

Tongkat ali is a Southeast Asian root traditionally used for male vitality. Small randomized trials and a meta-analysis found modest rises in testosterone and improved erectile-function scores, usually at 200–400 mg of standardized extract a day.

Hawthorn Berry

Hawthorn is a traditional heart and circulation herb. Some trials suggest small benefits in mild heart failure symptoms; there's no direct evidence for erections.

Saw Palmetto

Saw palmetto berry extract is the most popular herbal remedy for an enlarged prostate (BPH). Early small trials looked promising, but the large, well-run STEP and CAMUS trials and a Cochrane review found it worked no better than placebo for urinary symptoms, even at triple doses.

Magnesium

Magnesium is a mineral involved in hundreds of enzyme reactions, including muscle and nerve function and blood sugar control. Many adults get less than recommended. Supplements can help low magnesium levels, sleep quality in some studies and, in larger doses, constipation.

The sales page lists these ingredients but no amounts. Its reference list also cites studies on DHEA, niacin and folate, though those don't appear in the ingredient list shown.
