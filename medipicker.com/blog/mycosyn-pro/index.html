What Is Mycosyn Pro?

Mycosyn Pro is a capsule sold to 'flush away nail fungus in seconds'. Its letter is written by an author who says they worked at a Chicago dermatology clinic, together with a research colleague.

The ingredients it names (horsetail, saw palmetto, fo-ti, spirulina, biotin, folate, alfalfa and barley grass) are a typical hair-loss formula, not an antifungal one.

Mycosyn Pro Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“Simple Indian Ritual Flushes Away Nail Fungus In Seconds”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • Nothing clears nail fungus 'in seconds'; even prescription treatment takes months while the nail grows out.
  • None of the ingredients has antifungal evidence for nails.
  • Fo-ti (He Shou Wu) has been linked to liver injury in several countries.
  • The author and colleague can't be verified.

Mycosyn Pro Ingredients

The official website names 9 key ingredients in Mycosyn Pro. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Horsetail

Horsetail (Equisetum) is a plant rich in silica. There's little evidence it improves bone or joint health.

Saw Palmetto

Saw palmetto berry extract is the most popular herbal remedy for an enlarged prostate (BPH). Early small trials looked promising, but the large, well-run STEP and CAMUS trials and a Cochrane review found it worked no better than placebo for urinary symptoms, even at triple doses.

Fo-Ti (He Shou Wu)

Fo-ti is a Chinese herb traditionally used to prevent graying and hair loss. There's no good clinical evidence for hair, nails or fungus.

Spirulina

Spirulina is a blue-green alga sold as a protein- and nutrient-rich 'superfood'. Small studies suggest modest effects on cholesterol; evidence for immunity is limited.

Vitamin C

Vitamin C is a water-soluble antioxidant vitamin. It is needed for collagen and immune function, but extra vitamin C has no proven effect on memory in people who eat fruit and vegetables.

Folate

Folate is a B vitamin needed for cell growth. It helps people who are deficient and is essential in pregnancy; it has no antifungal effect.

Biotin

Biotin (vitamin B7) helps the body use carbohydrates and fats. Deficiency is rare; extra biotin doesn't improve blood sugar in most people.

Alfalfa

Alfalfa is a legume sold as a nutrient-rich 'green' supplement. There's little evidence for health benefits as a supplement.

Barley Grass

Barley grass is the young leaf of the barley plant, sold as a greens powder. It provides small amounts of vitamins and fiber; there's no evidence for nail fungus.

The letter names these ingredients 'and more'; amounts aren't disclosed.

Is Mycosyn Pro Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Do a patch test before using any new oil or cream on your skin.
  • See a doctor about persistent fungal infections, rashes or sudden hair loss.
  • Stop use if you notice irritation.
  • Horsetail contains thiaminase, which breaks down vitamin B1; it has poisoned grazing animals and isn't recommended for long-term use.
  • Fo-ti (He Shou Wu) has been linked to cases of liver injury, and regulators in several countries have issued warnings.
  • Spirulina from untested sources can be contaminated with toxins; it isn't suitable for people with autoimmune disease or PKU.
  • High-dose biotin can distort lab results, including thyroid and heart (troponin) tests; tell your doctor before blood tests.
  • Alfalfa seeds and sprouts can trigger lupus-like flares in susceptible people and contain vitamin K, which affects warfarin.
