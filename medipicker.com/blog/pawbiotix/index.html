PawBiotix Ingredients

The official website names 10 key ingredients in PawBiotix. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Kelp

Kelp is a large seaweed used in supplements as a natural source of iodine. Iodine is essential for thyroid hormones, but most people in countries with iodized salt already get enough; extra iodine has no proven digestive benefit.

Chlorella

Chlorella is a green freshwater alga sold as a nutrient supplement. Small studies suggest modest effects on cholesterol and blood pressure; there's no evidence for vision.

Turmeric (Curcumin)

Turmeric's active compound, curcumin, is studied for inflammation and joint pain. Trials in people with knee arthritis show modest pain relief, comparable to some painkillers in a few studies; curcumin is poorly absorbed unless combined with fat or black pepper extract.

Fennel Seed

Fennel seed is a traditional remedy for gas and bloating. There is limited evidence for infant colic and digestion, and none for nail health.

L-Glutamine

L-glutamine is the most abundant amino acid in the body and a fuel for gut cells. There is no good evidence that supplemental glutamine improves memory in healthy people.

Chicory Root

Chicory root is a source of inulin fiber. As a prebiotic fiber it feeds gut bacteria; evidence for liver health is limited.

Flaxseed

Flaxseed provides fiber, lignans and the plant omega-3 ALA. Ground flaxseed has been shown to help constipation and slightly improve cholesterol and blood pressure in human trials.

Beef Liver Powder

Beef liver is a nutrient-dense ingredient rich in vitamin A, B12, iron and copper. It's mainly a palatable source of nutrients for dogs; too much can supply excess vitamin A.

Collagen

Collagen supplements provide protein fragments (peptides) from animal skin, bones or fish. Trials suggest collagen peptides may modestly improve skin elasticity and joint pain; the body breaks them down, so they don't travel to a specific organ like the inner ear.

Sodium Copper Chlorophyllin

Sodium copper chlorophyllin is a water-soluble form of chlorophyll used as a colorant and deodorizer. It's used to reduce odors, including in pet products; evidence for other benefits is limited.

The probiotic strains and CFU count aren't listed in text, and there's no dosing guide by body weight.

Is PawBiotix Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Ask your vet before giving any supplement, especially if your dog takes medication or is pregnant.
  • Follow the dosing for your dog's weight.
  • See your vet about any sudden change in appetite, energy or behavior.
  • Seaweeds such as kelp and bladderwrack contain iodine — people with thyroid conditions or on thyroid medication should check with a doctor first.
  • Turmeric/curcumin supplements may increase bleeding risk with blood thinners and have been linked to rare liver injury.

Our Verdict on PawBiotix
