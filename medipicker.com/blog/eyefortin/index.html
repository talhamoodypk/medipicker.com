What Is EyeFortin?

EyeFortin is a liquid supplement taken by mouth with a dropper, sold to support strong vision. The seller describes 'a proprietary formula of 10 eye health supporting plant extracts'.

Several of the named ingredients (huperzine A, L-tyrosine, niacin and forskolin) are brain or metabolism ingredients rather than the nutrients studied for eye health, such as lutein, zeaxanthin and omega-3s.

EyeFortin Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“The Natural Ultra Absorbable Dropper That Supports Strong Vision”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • The AREDS2 trials showed a specific formula (vitamins C and E, zinc, copper, lutein and zeaxanthin) slows advanced macular degeneration; EyeFortin contains none of the lutein/zeaxanthin component.
  • Forskolin has been tested as an eye drop for glaucoma pressure, not as an oral vision supplement.
  • Eyebright is a traditional eyewash herb with no clinical evidence for vision.
  • High-dose niacin can, rarely, cause swelling in the retina (niacin maculopathy).

EyeFortin Ingredients

The official website names 6 key ingredients in EyeFortin. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Eyebright

Eyebright (Euphrasia) is a herb traditionally used as an eyewash for irritated eyes. There are no good clinical trials showing it improves vision when taken by mouth.

Taurine

Taurine is an amino acid found in high amounts in the retina. It's essential for retinal health in animals, but there's no evidence that supplements improve vision in people who eat a normal diet.

Forskolin (Coleus forskohlii)

Forskolin is a compound from the coleus plant that raises cAMP in cells. Two small trials showed mixed results on body fat and no clear weight loss.

Huperzine A

Huperzine A is a compound from Chinese club moss that blocks the breakdown of acetylcholine, similar to some prescription Alzheimer's drugs. Chinese studies reported benefits, but a US National Institute on Aging trial found no improvement in Alzheimer's disease at standard doses.

L-Tyrosine

L-tyrosine is an amino acid the body uses to make dopamine, adrenaline and thyroid hormones. Studies suggest it may help mental performance under acute stress; there is no evidence it improves digestion or bowel movements.

Niacin (Vitamin B3)

Niacin is a B vitamin used at high doses to lower cholesterol. Nicotinamide (a form of B3) is being studied for glaucoma; regular niacin has no proven vision benefit.

The website names six of the ten ingredients, with no amounts.

Is EyeFortin Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Have regular eye exams and see an eye doctor about sudden vision changes.
  • Supplements don't replace glasses, contact lenses or prescribed treatment.
  • Check with your doctor if you take medication.
  • Huperzine A can slow the heart rate and interact with Alzheimer's medicines and anticholinergic drugs.
  • L-tyrosine can interact with thyroid medication and MAO-inhibitor antidepressants.
  • High-dose niacin causes flushing and, rarely, liver problems and swelling of the retina.

Our Verdict on EyeFortin

EyeFortin's formula doesn't match the research on eye health. If you're worried about your vision, get a full eye exam; if you have macular degeneration, ask whether an AREDS2 supplement is right for you. Sudden vision changes need urgent care.
