RevaNail Ingredients

The official website names 4 key ingredients in RevaNail. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Cinnamon

Cinnamon bark (usually cassia) is a common spice studied for blood sugar. Meta-analyses show small, inconsistent reductions in fasting blood sugar; it doesn't meaningfully lower HbA1c.

Gymnema Sylvestre

Gymnema is an Ayurvedic herb known as 'sugar destroyer' because it blocks the taste of sweetness. Small trials suggest modest reductions in blood sugar in people with diabetes; quality is low.

Green Tea Extract

Green tea extract supplies catechins, mainly EGCG, and usually some caffeine. Studies show small effects on energy expenditure and fat oxidation, especially combined with caffeine; weight loss in trials is modest (around 1 kg).

Theobromine

Theobromine is a mild stimulant found in cocoa, related to caffeine. It has mild effects on alertness and blood vessels; there is no evidence for nail health.

The website names cinnamon, gymnema, green tea and theobromine plus 'a powerful blend of vitamins and minerals', without amounts.

Is RevaNail Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Do a patch test before using any new oil or cream on your skin.
  • See a doctor about persistent fungal infections, rashes or sudden hair loss.
  • Stop use if you notice irritation.
  • Cassia cinnamon contains coumarin, which can harm the liver in large regular amounts.
  • Gymnema can lower blood sugar and may add to the effect of diabetes medicine.
  • Concentrated green tea extract has been linked to rare cases of liver injury, especially when taken on an empty stomach; it also contains caffeine.
