NeuroTest Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“Perform This 16-Second Morning Routine To Get Thick, Hard Erections Whenever You Want... No Matter Your Age”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • Tongkat ali (including LJ100) raised testosterone and improved sexual-function scores in several small trials, mainly in men with low testosterone.
  • Ashwagandha has small trials showing modest testosterone increases in stressed men.
  • Mucuna contains L-dopa, a powerful compound that interacts with many medicines; the fertility study cited was in infertile men.
  • 'Hard erections whenever you want, no matter your age' overstates what these herbs achieve; prescription treatments are far more effective.

NeuroTest Ingredients

The official website names 4 key ingredients in NeuroTest. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Tongkat Ali (Eurycoma longifolia)

Tongkat ali is a Southeast Asian root traditionally used for male vitality. Small randomized trials and a meta-analysis found modest rises in testosterone and improved erectile-function scores, usually at 200–400 mg of standardized extract a day.

Mucuna Pruriens

Mucuna pruriens (velvet bean) is a natural source of L-dopa, the precursor of dopamine used to treat Parkinson's disease. It has been studied in Parkinson's disease under medical supervision; there's no evidence for tinnitus.

Ashwagandha

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) is an Ayurvedic herb classed as an adaptogen. Several small randomized trials found it lowered perceived stress and cortisol and improved sleep at 250–600 mg of root extract a day.

Krachaidum (Black Ginger)

Krachaidum (Kaempferia parviflora) is a Thai root traditionally used for male vitality. Small trials suggest modest improvements in erectile-function scores and physical performance.

Unlike most products in this category, NeuroTest names a branded, studied extract (LJ100 tongkat ali) and cites the trials. Amounts per capsule aren't given in text.

Is NeuroTest Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Talk to your doctor before use if you take medication, are pregnant or nursing, or have a health condition.
  • Don't exceed the recommended dose on the label.
  • Stop use if you notice any unusual reaction.
  • Mucuna contains L-dopa, which interacts with antidepressants (MAO inhibitors), blood pressure and psychiatric medicines and can cause nausea, low blood pressure and involuntary movements.
  • Ashwagandha isn't recommended in pregnancy, with thyroid medication or for people with autoimmune conditions; rare cases of liver injury have been reported.
