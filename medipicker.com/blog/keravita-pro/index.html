Keravita Pro Ingredients

The official website names 6 key ingredients in Keravita Pro. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Turmeric (Curcumin)

Turmeric's active compound, curcumin, is studied for inflammation and joint pain. Trials in people with knee arthritis show modest pain relief, comparable to some painkillers in a few studies; curcumin is poorly absorbed unless combined with fat or black pepper extract.

Cat's Claw

Cat's claw (Uncaria tomentosa) is a vine bark from the Amazon used for inflammation. Small studies explored it for arthritis; there is no evidence for prostate health.

Garlic

Garlic contains allicin and other sulfur compounds. Garlic supplements modestly lower blood pressure and cholesterol in trials; effects on blood sugar are small.

Quercetin

Quercetin is a plant flavonoid found in onions, apples and tea. Small studies suggest possible benefits for chronic prostatitis pain and blood pressure, but evidence is limited.

Pomegranate Extract

Pomegranate is rich in antioxidant ellagitannins. Pomegranate juice was studied in men with rising PSA after prostate cancer treatment; a placebo-controlled trial found no significant benefit.

Olive Leaf Extract

Olive leaf extract is rich in the polyphenol oleuropein. Small studies suggest modest effects on blood pressure and blood sugar; there is no evidence for memory.

The letter names these ingredients 'such as'; the full label and amounts aren't shown.
