Dentitox Pro Ingredients

The official website names 10 key ingredients in Dentitox Pro. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Vitamin A

Vitamin A supports the skin, gums and the lining of the mouth. Deficiency is rare in developed countries; extra vitamin A doesn't improve oral health in people who aren't deficient.

Vitamin C

Vitamin C is a water-soluble antioxidant vitamin. It is needed for collagen and immune function, but extra vitamin C has no proven effect on memory in people who eat fruit and vegetables.

Vitamin D

Vitamin D is needed for calcium absorption and bone health. It helps people who are deficient; the large D2d trial found it didn't significantly prevent type 2 diabetes in people with prediabetes.

Vitamin K2

Vitamin K2 helps direct calcium into bones and teeth. Evidence for dental benefits is mostly theoretical; it's studied for bone health.

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral found in high concentrations in the prostate. Supplements help if you are deficient, but there is no good evidence that extra zinc improves BPH symptoms; long-term high doses can cause copper deficiency.

Calcium

Calcium is the main mineral in teeth and bones. Adequate calcium matters for bones; supplements don't remineralize enamel the way topical fluoride does.

Xylitol

Xylitol is a sugar alcohol that cavity-causing bacteria can't use for energy. Chewing xylitol gum several times a day (around 5–10 g in total) can reduce cavity-causing bacteria and plaque.

Collagen

Collagen supplements provide protein fragments (peptides) from animal skin, bones or fish. Trials suggest collagen peptides may modestly improve skin elasticity and joint pain; the body breaks them down, so they don't travel to a specific organ like the inner ear.

MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)

MSM is a sulfur compound sold for joints and skin. Small trials suggest modest joint-pain benefits; there's no evidence for teeth.

Peppermint Oil

Peppermint oil relaxes the smooth muscle of the gut. Enteric-coated peppermint oil capsules have good evidence for irritable bowel syndrome symptoms; there's no evidence for blood sugar.

The letter also lists phosphorus, potassium and spearmint. Calcium is described as 'micro-encapsulated calcium (MEC)'. Amounts aren't disclosed.

Is Dentitox Pro Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Keep brushing, flossing and visiting your dentist — supplements don't replace dental care.
  • See a dentist about tooth pain, bleeding gums or swelling.
  • Products containing xylitol must be kept away from dogs.
  • High-dose vitamin A can be toxic and isn't safe in pregnancy.
  • Vitamin K interferes with warfarin; people on warfarin should avoid K supplements unless their doctor approves.
  • Xylitol is extremely toxic to dogs, so keep xylitol products away from pets; large amounts can cause diarrhea in people.
  • Peppermint oil can worsen heartburn and reflux.
