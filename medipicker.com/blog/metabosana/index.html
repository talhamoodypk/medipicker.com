What Is MetaboSana?

MetaboSana is a German-language metabolism and weight capsule that claims to activate the body's own GLP-1 production, positioning itself against weight-loss injections. It's part of the same '-Sana' product family as CleanseSana.

MetaboSana Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“Was wäre, wenn deutsche Forscher einen Weg gefunden haben, Ihre körpereigene GLP-1-Produktion zu aktivieren? (What if German researchers found a way to activate your own GLP-1 production?)”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • No supplement has been shown to raise GLP-1 to anything like the levels produced by semaglutide or tirzepatide injections.
  • Berberine at 50 mg is far below studied doses, and banaba at 1 mg and ginseng at 2.5 mg are token amounts.
  • The page claims cayenne and EGCG give '340% stronger thermogenesis', which isn't a figure from human research.
  • Publishing doses is good practice, and it lets readers see how small they are.

MetaboSana Ingredients

The official website names 10 key ingredients in MetaboSana. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Alpha-Lipoic Acid

Alpha-lipoic acid (ALA) is an antioxidant made in the body and used as a supplement for nerve pain. Trials in diabetic neuropathy show modest improvement in burning and tingling at 600 mg a day; it's prescribed for this in Germany.

Green Tea Extract

Green tea extract supplies catechins, mainly EGCG, and usually some caffeine. Studies show small effects on energy expenditure and fat oxidation, especially combined with caffeine; weight loss in trials is modest (around 1 kg).

Berberine

Berberine is a yellow compound found in barberry, goldenseal and Chinese goldthread. Meta-analyses of randomized trials show it lowers fasting blood sugar, HbA1c and LDL cholesterol, usually at 900–1,500 mg a day split into doses; it often causes stomach upset.

Chromium

Chromium is a trace mineral involved in insulin action. Meta-analyses show at most small effects on blood sugar and weight; deficiency is rare.

Banaba Leaf

Banaba leaf contains corosolic acid, studied for blood sugar. A few small trials found modest reductions in blood sugar; larger studies are lacking.

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral found in high concentrations in the prostate. Supplements help if you are deficient, but there is no good evidence that extra zinc improves BPH symptoms; long-term high doses can cause copper deficiency.

Milk Thistle (Silymarin)

Milk thistle seed extract contains silymarin, the most widely used liver-support herb. Trials in fatty liver disease and hepatitis show small or inconsistent improvements in liver enzymes; it's generally well tolerated.

Resveratrol

Resveratrol is a polyphenol in grapes and berries, made famous by 'red wine' longevity research. Human trials have been disappointing, with small or no effects on heart health, blood sugar and aging.

Ginseng

Ginseng (Panax) root is a traditional energy and vitality herb. Some trials show small improvements in fatigue and erectile-function scores in people; evidence quality is mixed.

Cayenne Pepper

Cayenne contains capsaicin, the compound that makes chili peppers hot. Capsaicin slightly raises energy use and may reduce appetite in studies, but the effects are small; it can also irritate a sensitive stomach.

MetaboSana publishes its doses per capsule: alpha-lipoic acid 75 mg, green tea EGCG 75 mg, berberine HCl 50 mg, chromium picolinate 50 mcg, banaba leaf 1 mg, zinc gluconate 5.5 mg, milk thistle 50 mg, resveratrol 20 mg, Korean ginseng 2.5 mg and cayenne 2.5 mg. Studies of berberine used 1,000–1,500 mg a day, 20–30 times more than one capsule provides.

Is MetaboSana Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Some weight management formulas contain caffeine or green tea extract — avoid taking them late in the day if you're sensitive.
  • Talk to your doctor first if you take medication for diabetes, blood pressure or your heart.
  • Don't use weight-loss supplements while pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Alpha-lipoic acid can lower blood sugar; people with diabetes should monitor their levels.
  • Concentrated green tea extract has been linked to rare cases of liver injury, especially when taken on an empty stomach; it also contains caffeine.
  • Berberine can add to the effect of diabetes medicine and interacts with drugs cleared by the liver (such as cyclosporine); avoid it in pregnancy.
  • Ginseng can affect blood sugar and blood pressure and interacts with warfarin.
  • Cayenne/capsaicin can cause heartburn or stomach irritation in sensitive people.

Our Verdict on MetaboSana

MetaboSana deserves credit for transparency, but the doses it publishes show it can't do what it promises. Anyone interested in GLP-1 medicines should discuss them with a doctor, and anyone wanting berberine should know that studied doses are much higher (and interact with diabetes medicine).
