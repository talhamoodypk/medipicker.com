What Is FlowForce Max?

FlowForce Max is a prostate formula that, unusually, blames prostate and urinary problems on fungal infection. The seller says it helps keep the bladder and urinary tract safe from fungi.

Its reference list includes case reports of Candida prostatitis and news stories about fungi in sink drains. Fungal prostatitis does exist, but it is rare and mostly seen in people with weakened immune systems; it isn't a common cause of an enlarged prostate.

FlowForce Max Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“The Natural Formula That Supports Prostate Health”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • An enlarged prostate (BPH) is caused by hormone-driven growth with age, not by fungus in most men.
  • Oregano extract kills fungi in lab tests, but there's no evidence it treats urinary or prostate infections.
  • Muira puama is a Brazilian 'libido' herb with very limited human research.
  • Saw palmetto did no better than placebo in the largest prostate trials.

FlowForce Max Ingredients

The official website names 5 key ingredients in FlowForce Max. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Saw Palmetto

Saw palmetto berry extract is the most popular herbal remedy for an enlarged prostate (BPH). Early small trials looked promising, but the large, well-run STEP and CAMUS trials and a Cochrane review found it worked no better than placebo for urinary symptoms, even at triple doses.

Muira Puama

Muira puama is a Brazilian bark traditionally used as an aphrodisiac. Human evidence is limited to a few old, low-quality studies.

Oregano Leaf Extract

Oregano extract contains carvacrol and thymol, natural antimicrobials. It kills fungi and bacteria in lab tests; there's no clinical evidence for prostate or urinary health.

Grape Seed Extract

Grape seed extract is rich in antioxidant proanthocyanidins. It may modestly lower blood pressure; protection against hearing loss has only been shown in animal studies.

Peppermint Oil

Peppermint oil relaxes the smooth muscle of the gut. Enteric-coated peppermint oil capsules have good evidence for irritable bowel syndrome symptoms; there's no evidence for blood sugar.

The label also lists magnesium stearate (a manufacturing flow agent). Amounts are not disclosed.
