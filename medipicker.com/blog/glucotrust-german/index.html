GlucoTrust (German) Ingredients

The official website names 11 key ingredients in GlucoTrust (German). Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Gymnema Sylvestre

Gymnema is an Ayurvedic herb known as 'sugar destroyer' because it blocks the taste of sweetness. Small trials suggest modest reductions in blood sugar in people with diabetes; quality is low.

Chromium

Chromium is a trace mineral involved in insulin action. Meta-analyses show at most small effects on blood sugar and weight; deficiency is rare.

Biotin

Biotin (vitamin B7) helps the body use carbohydrates and fats. Deficiency is rare; extra biotin doesn't improve blood sugar in most people.

Cinnamon

Cinnamon bark (usually cassia) is a common spice studied for blood sugar. Meta-analyses show small, inconsistent reductions in fasting blood sugar; it doesn't meaningfully lower HbA1c.

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral found in high concentrations in the prostate. Supplements help if you are deficient, but there is no good evidence that extra zinc improves BPH symptoms; long-term high doses can cause copper deficiency.

Manganese

Manganese is a trace mineral involved in metabolism. Deficiency is rare and extra manganese has no proven blood sugar benefit.

Licorice Root

Licorice root contains glycyrrhizin, which gives it its sweet taste. It is used for indigestion and coughs; there is no evidence for memory.

Juniper Berry

Juniper berries are used as a flavoring (in gin) and as a traditional diuretic. There is no clinical evidence for blood sugar, blood pressure or urinary health.

Guggul

Guggul is a resin from the Commiphora mukul tree used in Ayurveda for cholesterol. A well-run US trial found it didn't lower cholesterol, and slightly raised LDL in some people.

Turmeric (Curcumin)

Turmeric's active compound, curcumin, is studied for inflammation and joint pain. Trials in people with knee arthritis show modest pain relief, comparable to some painkillers in a few studies; curcumin is poorly absorbed unless combined with fat or black pepper extract.

Vitamin D

Vitamin D is needed for calcium absorption and bone health. It helps people who are deficient; the large D2d trial found it didn't significantly prevent type 2 diabetes in people with prediabetes.

Amounts are not given in text. The German page lists these nutrients and cites general research on each (for example a 2003 cinnamon study in Diabetes Care).

Is GlucoTrust (German) Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • If you have diabetes or take insulin or glucose-lowering medicine, speak to your doctor first — some ingredients can lower blood sugar further.
  • Keep monitoring your blood sugar as advised by your doctor; supplements don't replace prescribed treatment.
  • Avoid during pregnancy or breastfeeding unless your doctor approves.
  • Gymnema can lower blood sugar and may add to the effect of diabetes medicine.
  • High-dose biotin can distort lab results, including thyroid and heart (troponin) tests; tell your doctor before blood tests.
  • Cassia cinnamon contains coumarin, which can harm the liver in large regular amounts.
  • Licorice containing glycyrrhizin can raise blood pressure and lower potassium with regular use.
  • Juniper isn't recommended in pregnancy or for people with kidney disease.
  • Guggul can interact with thyroid medicine, blood thinners and birth control.
  • Turmeric/curcumin supplements may increase bleeding risk with blood thinners and have been linked to rare liver injury.
