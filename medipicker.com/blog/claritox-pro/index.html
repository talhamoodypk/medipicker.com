What Is Claritox Pro?

Claritox Pro is a capsule supplement sold to support the brain and the balance system and to 'prevent dizziness'. The sales letter is written by a 67-year-old creator who says he researched plants for a decade before making the formula.

Dizziness has many causes, including inner-ear problems such as BPPV, low blood pressure, dehydration, anemia, medication side effects and heart rhythm problems, and most need a medical diagnosis rather than a supplement.

Claritox Pro Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“One Simple Way To Maintain Your Balance And Prevent Dizziness!”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • No ingredient list is published, so the 'balance' claims can't be checked against any research.
  • No study of Claritox Pro is cited.
  • 'Prevent dizziness' is a medical claim; recurrent dizziness should be assessed by a doctor because some causes are serious.
  • The page uses 'limited stock' urgency to push 3- and 6-bottle orders.

Claritox Pro Ingredients

The Claritox Pro sales page and website do not list the ingredients anywhere in text. That makes it impossible to judge the formula, check doses or rule out interactions before buying. Ask the seller for the supplement facts label before ordering.

How to Take Claritox Pro

The website does not state the daily dose in text; follow the instructions on the bottle.
