What Is Emperor's Vigor Tonic?

Emperor's Vigor Tonic is a once-a-day capsule sold to boost men's energy, virility and libido, using a proprietary formula of 11 plant extracts inspired by Chinese imperial medicine.

Its 11 ingredients are exactly the same as those of DigestiStart, which is sold for digestion. The formula itself is a traditional Chinese 'kidney yang' tonic, and in TCM that is closer to Emperor's Vigor Tonic's purpose than to DigestiStart's.

Emperor's Vigor Tonic Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“Supports Men's Virility And Libido”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • Cnidium and cistanche are used for libido in TCM, but human trials are almost non-existent.
  • The same formula is sold elsewhere for digestion, which shows how loosely these products are marketed.
  • The claim that all ingredients are 'proven to be safe in clinical trials' isn't backed by cited trials.
  • Imported TCM herbs have sometimes been found contaminated with heavy metals or undeclared drugs.

Emperor's Vigor Tonic Ingredients

The official website names 11 key ingredients in Emperor's Vigor Tonic. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Rehmannia Root

Rehmannia is a core herb of traditional Chinese 'kidney tonic' formulas. Research is mostly in animals; there's no clinical evidence for digestion.

Cornus Fruit (Shan Zhu Yu)

Cornus (Asiatic dogwood) fruit is a traditional Chinese tonic herb. Evidence is limited to lab and animal studies.

Wild Yam (Dioscorea)

Wild yam root contains diosgenin, a plant compound used in labs to make hormones. The body can't turn wild yam into hormones, and there's no good evidence for digestion.

Poria Cocos

Poria is a fungus used in traditional Chinese medicine as a calming diuretic. Human evidence is minimal.

Cistanche

Cistanche is a desert plant used in TCM as a 'yang' tonic for fatigue and libido. Studies are mostly in animals.

Dodder Seed (Cuscuta)

Dodder seed is a TCM herb used for kidney and reproductive health. There's very little human evidence.

Eucommia Bark

Eucommia bark is used in TCM for back pain and blood pressure. A few small studies suggest a mild blood-pressure effect; nothing for digestion.

Achyranthes Root

Achyranthes is a TCM herb for joints and circulation. Evidence is mostly from animal studies.

Polygala Root

Polygala tenuifolia is used in TCM for memory and calm. Small studies suggest possible memory effects; it can cause stomach upset.

Schisandra Berry

Schisandra is an adaptogenic berry used in Chinese and Russian traditional medicine. Small studies suggest possible effects on fatigue and liver enzymes; evidence is limited.

Cnidium Monnieri

Cnidium seed contains osthole and is used in TCM for libido and skin itching. Evidence is mostly from lab and animal studies.

All ingredients are in a proprietary blend without amounts.

Is Emperor's Vigor Tonic Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Talk to your doctor before use if you take blood pressure, heart or prostate medication.
  • See a doctor about any new urinary symptoms — they can have causes that need medical care.
  • Don't exceed the recommended dose on the label.
  • Several traditional Chinese herbs, including achyranthes, are not recommended in pregnancy.
  • Schisandra can change how the liver processes some medicines (such as tacrolimus).

Our Verdict on Emperor's Vigor Tonic

Emperor's Vigor Tonic is a classic Chinese tonic with little modern evidence. It's unlikely to do much for erections or libido. Low libido and erection problems can be linked to low testosterone, diabetes, heart disease or medications, so see a doctor; effective treatments exist.
