TitanFlow Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“TitanFlow from Zenith Labs, presented by Dr. Ryan Shelton”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • Pumpkin seed oil, beta-sitosterol and pygeum are among the better-supported herbal options for urinary symptoms of BPH, with modest benefits in trials.
  • The page claims flow improved 'by over 20%'; trial improvements are real but small compared with prescription drugs.
  • The claim that 'deep sea divers' eat pumpkin seeds every day is an odd marketing line with no relevance.
  • Lycopene and broccoli sprouts have no direct evidence for urinary flow.

TitanFlow Ingredients

The official website names 5 key ingredients in TitanFlow. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Pumpkin Seed Extract

Pumpkin seed provides zinc, plant sterols and healthy fats. Some trials suggest pumpkin seed extract helps urinary symptoms from an enlarged prostate; there's no evidence for nails or feet.

Plant Sterol Complex (Beta-Sitosterol)

Plant sterols such as beta-sitosterol are cholesterol-like compounds found in nuts, seeds and vegetable oils. Beta-sitosterol improved urinary flow and symptom scores in a Cochrane review of four trials, though it did not shrink the prostate; sterols also modestly lower LDL cholesterol.

Lycopene (Tomato Fruit Powder)

Lycopene is the red carotenoid in tomatoes. Diets high in tomato products are linked with lower prostate cancer risk in observational studies, but supplement trials have not shown a clear benefit for BPH symptoms.

Broccoli Leaf Extract

Broccoli extracts provide glucosinolates that the body converts into sulforaphane. Sulforaphane is being studied for cancer prevention, but there are no trials showing benefits for prostate symptoms.

Pygeum Africanum Bark

Pygeum is an extract of the African plum tree bark used in Europe for BPH. A Cochrane review of older, small trials found a moderate improvement in night-time urination and urine flow; high-quality modern trials are lacking.

The page explains the role of each ingredient; amounts per capsule aren't given in text.
