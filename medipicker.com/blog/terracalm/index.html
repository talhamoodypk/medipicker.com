What Is TerraCalm?

TerraCalm Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“This French Antifungal Mineral Mud Supports Healthy Toenails”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • Tea tree oil has the best evidence here: one older trial found 100% tea tree oil worked about as well as clotrimazole cream for toenail fungus, though both cleared only a minority of nails.
  • Thyme and oregano oils kill fungi in lab dishes, but there are no good clinical trials on toenails.
  • Clay has no proven antifungal effect on nails; the 'starving the fungus' explanation on the sales page isn't backed by research.
  • Topical products struggle to reach fungus under the nail; prescription topical and oral antifungals still have the best cure rates.

TerraCalm Ingredients

The official website names 9 key ingredients in TerraCalm. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

French Green Clay

French green clay (illite or montmorillonite) is an absorbent mineral clay used in face and foot masks. It absorbs oil and moisture, but there's no clinical evidence that it treats nail fungus.

Tea Tree Oil

Tea tree oil is an essential oil from the Australian Melaleuca alternifolia tree. One randomized trial found 100% tea tree oil applied twice daily for six months gave results similar to clotrimazole for toenail fungus, with full cure in only a minority of patients.

Oregano Essential Oil

Oregano oil contains carvacrol and thymol, which kill fungi in lab studies. There are no good clinical trials on nail fungus.

Thyme Essential Oil

Thyme oil is rich in thymol, an antiseptic compound used in mouthwashes. It has lab antifungal activity but no clinical trials for toenails.

Lavender Oil

Lavender essential oil is used for its scent and mild antimicrobial activity. Lab studies show some antifungal effects; human evidence for nail fungus is lacking.

Clove Bud Oil

Clove oil is rich in eugenol, a numbing and antiseptic compound. It shows antifungal activity in lab tests; clinical evidence on nails is lacking.

Manuka Honey

Manuka honey from New Zealand has antibacterial properties and is used in medical wound dressings. It has evidence for wound care, but not for fungal nail infections.

Aloe Vera

Aloe vera gel and latex come from the leaves of the aloe plant. Aloe latex is a strong stimulant laxative; the inner gel has limited, mixed evidence for irritable bowel symptoms.

Vitamin E

Vitamin E is a fat-soluble antioxidant vitamin. The large SELECT trial found vitamin E supplements slightly increased prostate cancer risk in men, and it has shown no clear benefit for memory in healthy adults.

The jar also contains cedarwood essential oil, bearberry extract, jojoba oil, shea butter and menthol. Amounts are not disclosed.

Is TerraCalm Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Do a patch test before using any new oil or cream on your skin.
  • See a doctor about persistent fungal infections, rashes or sudden hair loss.
  • Stop use if you notice irritation.
  • Essential oils such as tea tree, oregano, thyme and clove can irritate or sensitize skin; test a small area first and never swallow them.
  • Aloe latex is a stimulant laxative and isn't suitable for long-term daily use or during pregnancy.
