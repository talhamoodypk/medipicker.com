NewEra Protect Claims vs. Evidence

  • There's no evidence that resveratrol, horsetail or sumac 'tone' the pelvic floor or bladder.
  • The references cited are about resveratrol and kidney function, which is a different organ and a different problem.

NewEra Protect Ingredients

The official website names 6 key ingredients in NewEra Protect. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Boswellia

Boswellia (Indian frankincense) resin is used for inflammation and joint pain. Small trials suggest it may reduce osteoarthritis pain; there's no evidence for bladder control.

Horsetail

Horsetail (Equisetum) is a plant rich in silica. There's little evidence it improves bone or joint health.

Sumac Extract

Sumac is a tangy Middle Eastern spice rich in polyphenols. Small studies look at blood sugar and cholesterol; there's no evidence for bladder health.

Resveratrol

Resveratrol is a polyphenol in grapes and berries, made famous by 'red wine' longevity research. Human trials have been disappointing, with small or no effects on heart health, blood sugar and aging.

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral found in high concentrations in the prostate. Supplements help if you are deficient, but there is no good evidence that extra zinc improves BPH symptoms; long-term high doses can cause copper deficiency.

Vitamin D

Vitamin D is needed for calcium absorption and bone health. It helps people who are deficient; the large D2d trial found it didn't significantly prevent type 2 diabetes in people with prediabetes.

The website says resveratrol, horsetail and sumac 'tone the tissues that support your bladder'. Amounts aren't disclosed.
