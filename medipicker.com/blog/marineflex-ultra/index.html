MarineFlex Ultra Ingredients

The official website names 4 key ingredients in MarineFlex Ultra. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Green-Lipped Mussel

Green-lipped mussel extract comes from a New Zealand shellfish and contains omega-3 fatty acids. Small trials suggest it may modestly reduce osteoarthritis pain and stiffness; evidence quality is mixed.

Boswellia

Boswellia (Indian frankincense) resin is used for inflammation and joint pain. Small trials suggest it may reduce osteoarthritis pain; there's no evidence for bladder control.

Hyaluronic Acid

Hyaluronic acid is a natural lubricant found in joint fluid and skin. Small trials of oral hyaluronic acid suggest modest relief of knee pain; injected forms are better studied.

Glucosamine

Glucosamine is a building block of cartilage, usually made from shellfish. Results are mixed: some European trials of glucosamine sulfate showed small benefits, while the large US GAIT trial found no clear benefit over placebo.

Amounts aren't given in text. The page argues fish oil carries a risk of mercury poisoning; purified fish oil supplements are tested and generally low in mercury.
