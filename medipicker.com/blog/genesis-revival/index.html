Genesis Revival Ingredients

The official website names 6 key ingredients in Genesis Revival. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Shilajit

Shilajit is a tar-like mineral resin from mountain rocks used in Ayurveda. Small trials suggest it may raise testosterone and sperm count slightly.

Turmeric (Curcumin)

Turmeric's active compound, curcumin, is studied for inflammation and joint pain. Trials in people with knee arthritis show modest pain relief, comparable to some painkillers in a few studies; curcumin is poorly absorbed unless combined with fat or black pepper extract.

Schisandra Berry

Schisandra is an adaptogenic berry used in Chinese and Russian traditional medicine. Small studies suggest possible effects on fatigue and liver enzymes; evidence is limited.

Lion's Mane Mushroom

Lion's mane (Hericium erinaceus) is a mushroom studied for nerve growth factor. A few small human trials suggest mild benefits for memory in older adults; it isn't a digestive ingredient.

Resveratrol

Resveratrol is a polyphenol in grapes and berries, made famous by 'red wine' longevity research. Human trials have been disappointing, with small or no effects on heart health, blood sugar and aging.

Reishi Mushroom

Reishi (Ganoderma lucidum) is a mushroom used in traditional Chinese medicine. One small Japanese trial found a modest improvement in urinary symptom scores; larger studies are needed.

Each ingredient is linked to an ancient origin story (for example grape skin 'from Noah's vineyards'). Amounts are not disclosed.
