What Is HoneyBurn?

HoneyBurn is a liquid honey-based weight-loss supplement taken with a dropper. The seller calls it 'the first and only purple honey designed to boost lipase levels'.

HoneyBurn Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“Purple Weight Loss Honey Activates Your Metabolic Switch With Incredible Results”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • 'Boosting lipase' isn't a recognized way to lose weight; lipase is the enzyme that digests fat in the gut.
  • Berberine is the only ingredient with modest weight evidence (about 2 kg in meta-analyses at 1–1.5 g a day), and its dose here is unknown.
  • Kudzu has been studied for reducing alcohol intake, not weight.
  • Bee pollen, royal jelly and propolis can cause serious allergic reactions in people allergic to bees or pollen.

HoneyBurn Ingredients

The official website names 8 key ingredients in HoneyBurn. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Berberine

Berberine is a yellow compound found in barberry, goldenseal and Chinese goldthread. Meta-analyses of randomized trials show it lowers fasting blood sugar, HbA1c and LDL cholesterol, usually at 900–1,500 mg a day split into doses; it often causes stomach upset.

Kudzu Root

Kudzu is a vine whose root contains isoflavones such as puerarin. Small studies found it reduced alcohol intake in heavy drinkers; there's no good evidence for weight loss.

Raw Wildflower Honey

Honey is a natural sweetener made mostly of fructose and glucose. It has some antibacterial properties, but as a sugar it doesn't promote weight loss.

Holy Basil (Tulsi)

Holy basil is an Ayurvedic herb used as an adaptogen. A handful of small trials suggest it may reduce stress and anxiety scores; the studies are short and of variable quality.

Olive Leaf Extract

Olive leaf extract is rich in the polyphenol oleuropein. Small studies suggest modest effects on blood pressure and blood sugar; there is no evidence for memory.

Bee Pollen

Bee pollen is pollen collected by bees, sold as a 'superfood'. There's no reliable evidence it helps with weight loss.

Royal Jelly

Royal jelly is a secretion used by bees to feed queen bees. Small studies explored it for cholesterol and menopausal symptoms; there's no evidence for weight loss.

Propolis

Propolis is a resin bees use to seal their hives. It has some evidence for cold sores and wound healing when applied to the skin; none for weight loss.

Amounts are not disclosed. Raw honey is mostly sugar, so the honey base adds a small number of calories with each serving.

Is HoneyBurn Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Some weight management formulas contain caffeine or green tea extract — avoid taking them late in the day if you're sensitive.
  • Talk to your doctor first if you take medication for diabetes, blood pressure or your heart.
  • Don't use weight-loss supplements while pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Berberine can add to the effect of diabetes medicine and interacts with drugs cleared by the liver (such as cyclosporine); avoid it in pregnancy.
  • Bee products (pollen, royal jelly, propolis) can cause severe allergic reactions, including anaphylaxis, in people with pollen or bee allergies or asthma.
