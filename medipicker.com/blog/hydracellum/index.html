Hydracellum Ingredients

The official website names 5 key ingredients in Hydracellum. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Japanese Witch Hazel

Witch hazel is an astringent plant extract used in toners. It can soothe minor irritation and reduce oiliness; it doesn't reverse wrinkles.

Aloe Vera

Aloe vera gel and latex come from the leaves of the aloe plant. Aloe latex is a strong stimulant laxative; the inner gel has limited, mixed evidence for irritable bowel symptoms.

Jojoba Oil

Jojoba oil is a liquid wax similar to the skin's natural oils. It's an effective, non-greasy moisturizer; it doesn't firm skin.

Gotu Kola (Centella Asiatica)

Gotu kola (centella) is a herb widely used in skincare as 'cica'. It has evidence for supporting wound healing and soothing irritated skin; anti-wrinkle evidence is modest.

Green Tea Extract

Green tea extract supplies catechins, mainly EGCG, and usually some caffeine. Studies show small effects on energy expenditure and fat oxidation, especially combined with caffeine; weight loss in trials is modest (around 1 kg).

The letter names these plant extracts 'and more'; the full ingredient list and concentrations aren't shown in text.

Is Hydracellum Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Talk to your doctor before use if you take medication, are pregnant or nursing, or have a health condition.
  • Don't exceed the recommended dose on the label.
  • Stop use if you notice any unusual reaction.
  • Aloe latex is a stimulant laxative and isn't suitable for long-term daily use or during pregnancy.
  • Concentrated green tea extract has been linked to rare cases of liver injury, especially when taken on an empty stomach; it also contains caffeine.
