What Is NerveSana?

NerveSana is a German-language nerve-pain capsule from the '-Sana' product family, with alpha-lipoic acid, magnesium bisglycinate, curcuminoids and CoQ10. The page criticizes prescription nerve-pain drugs (gabapentin and pregabalin) as 'numbing you like a zombie'.

It cites Ikaria research by a 'Dr. Elena Papadakis, neurologist at the University of Athens', whom we couldn't verify. It also quotes a symptom-score result (63.5% vs 38.4% placebo) that resembles published alpha-lipoic acid trials rather than a study of NerveSana.

NerveSana Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“Was kein Neurologe sagt: 3 Enzyme & Ihr Nervenbrennen (What no neurologist tells you: 3 enzymes and your burning nerves)”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • Alpha-lipoic acid at 600 mg a day has genuine evidence for diabetic nerve pain (the SYDNEY and ALADIN trials).
  • Whether NerveSana provides 600 mg isn't stated.
  • Discouraging prescribed medicines like gabapentin and pregabalin is risky; stopping them suddenly can cause withdrawal and seizures.
  • The 'raw material shortage' and 'waiting list of 247 people' are pressure tactics.

NerveSana Ingredients

The official website names 4 key ingredients in NerveSana. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Alpha-Lipoic Acid

Alpha-lipoic acid (ALA) is an antioxidant made in the body and used as a supplement for nerve pain. Trials in diabetic neuropathy show modest improvement in burning and tingling at 600 mg a day; it's prescribed for this in Germany.

Magnesium

Magnesium is a mineral involved in hundreds of enzyme reactions, including muscle and nerve function and blood sugar control. Many adults get less than recommended. Supplements can help low magnesium levels, sleep quality in some studies and, in larger doses, constipation.

Turmeric (Curcumin)

Turmeric's active compound, curcumin, is studied for inflammation and joint pain. Trials in people with knee arthritis show modest pain relief, comparable to some painkillers in a few studies; curcumin is poorly absorbed unless combined with fat or black pepper extract.

Coenzyme Q10

CoQ10 is an antioxidant involved in energy production in cells. It has some evidence for heart failure and may ease statin-related muscle aches in some people; there's no evidence for lung function.

Magnesium is in the bisglycinate form. Amounts aren't shown in text.

Is NerveSana Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • See a doctor about numbness, burning or weakness — these can be signs of conditions that need treatment.
  • Check with your doctor if you take blood thinners or diabetes medication.
  • Stop using the product if you notice any unusual reaction.
  • Alpha-lipoic acid can lower blood sugar; people with diabetes should monitor their levels.
  • Turmeric/curcumin supplements may increase bleeding risk with blood thinners and have been linked to rare liver injury.
  • CoQ10 may reduce the effect of warfarin.

Our Verdict on NerveSana

NerveSana's main ingredient is a sensible choice, but its marketing is not. Don't stop prescribed nerve-pain medicines without your doctor. If you want to try alpha-lipoic acid, ask your doctor about a standard 600 mg product, especially if you have diabetes, since it can lower blood sugar.
