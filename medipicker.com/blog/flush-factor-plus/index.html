What Is Flush Factor Plus?

Flush Factor Plus is a once-a-day capsule sold for swollen feet and ankles. The seller says it supports healthy fluid circulation in the legs and the body's natural ability to regulate fluids.

Flush Factor Plus Claims vs. Evidence

The official website leads with this headline:

“Swollen Feet and Ankles, Pain or Discomfort? Make sure to clear out all of your symptoms!”

Here is how the main claims hold up against published research and the information the seller actually provides:

  • Swollen ankles can be a sign of heart failure, kidney or liver disease, blood clots or medication side effects, so they need a medical check.
  • Hibiscus tea modestly lowers blood pressure in trials and has a mild diuretic effect.
  • Beetroot and citrulline support nitric oxide and blood flow, but there's no evidence they reduce leg swelling.
  • Pineapple provides bromelain, which has small studies for post-surgery swelling, at doses a blend is unlikely to reach.

Flush Factor Plus Ingredients

The official website names 5 key ingredients in Flush Factor Plus. Here is what each one is and what research says about it.

Pineapple Powder (Bromelain)

Pineapple contains bromelain, a protein-digesting enzyme. Bromelain has small studies for reducing swelling after surgery or injury, usually at concentrated doses.

L-Citrulline

L-citrulline is an amino acid the body converts into arginine and nitric oxide. It can modestly improve blood flow and exercise performance; there's no evidence it reduces leg swelling.

Black Cumin Seed (Nigella sativa)

Black seed is a traditional Middle Eastern remedy. Meta-analyses show modest improvements in blood pressure, cholesterol and blood sugar; there's no evidence for swelling.

Beetroot

Beetroot is rich in nitrates, which the body turns into nitric oxide. Beetroot juice can modestly lower blood pressure and help exercise performance; there's no evidence for skin.

Hibiscus Sabdariffa

Hibiscus flower is used to make a tart red tea. Trials show hibiscus tea modestly lowers blood pressure; it has a mild diuretic effect.

These ingredients are in a 'proprietary blend' with no amounts. L-citrulline is listed as L-citrulline DL-malate.

Is Flush Factor Plus Safe? Side Effects to Know

  • Some weight management formulas contain caffeine or green tea extract — avoid taking them late in the day if you're sensitive.
  • Talk to your doctor first if you take medication for diabetes, blood pressure or your heart.
  • Don't use weight-loss supplements while pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Bromelain may increase bleeding risk with blood thinners.
  • Hibiscus can add to the effect of blood pressure medicines.
