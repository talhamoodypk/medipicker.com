Possible Side Effects & Safety

The formula is made from plant extracts, and most healthy adults tolerate ingredients like these well. Still, active botanicals can cause side effects or interact with medicines. Here's what to know:

  • Low blood sugar risk. Gymnema and banaba can lower blood sugar. Combined with insulin or diabetes pills, this could push levels too low. Talk to your doctor before starting.
  • Blood thinning. Turmeric and pine bark extract may slow blood clotting. If you take blood thinners or have a bleeding disorder, ask your doctor first.
  • Blood pressure. Eleuthero and butcher's broom can affect blood pressure in some people. Check with your doctor if you have high blood pressure or take medication for it.
  • Stomach upset. Mild nausea or an upset stomach is possible. Taking the capsule with dinner, as directed, helps.
  • Pregnancy and kidneys. Juniper berry should be avoided during pregnancy and by people with kidney problems.

Stop taking it and contact your doctor if you feel shaky, dizzy, unusually tired or notice any other unusual reaction.

The FDA does not approve dietary supplements, so be cautious of any website that claims a supplement is "FDA approved."
